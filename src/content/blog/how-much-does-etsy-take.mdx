Etsy takes a listing fee plus a 6.5% transaction fee plus a payment processing fee that varies by country.

For a $50 US sale with $5 shipping, the core fees are roughly $0.20 listing, $3.58 transaction, and $1.90 payment processing, about $5.68 total. In the UK a £50 sale costs about £0.20 listing, £3.25 transaction, £2.20 processing, plus a 0.32% regulatory fee.

Try your own numbers in the Etsy Fee Calculator to see the exact split.
