Etsy charges several fees on each sale: a listing fee, a transaction fee, payment processing, and in some cases a regulatory operating fee, currency conversion fee, and Offsite Ads fee.

The listing fee is $0.20 per item. The transaction fee is 6.5% of the sale total including shipping and gift wrap. Payment processing varies by country, for example 3% + $0.25 in the US and 4% + £0.20 in the UK.
