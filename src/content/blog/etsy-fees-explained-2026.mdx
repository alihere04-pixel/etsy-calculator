Etsy charges several fees on each sale: a listing fee, a transaction fee, payment processing, and in some cases a regulatory operating fee, currency conversion fee, and Offsite Ads fee. This guide explains every one of them, with real numbers, so you know exactly what Etsy takes before you price a single item. Source: Etsy Fees & Payments Policy.

## Every Fee at a Glance

- **Listing fee:** $0.20 per item, charged when you publish and on every renewal.
- **Transaction fee:** 6.5% of the order total, including shipping and gift wrap.
- **Payment processing:** a percentage plus a fixed fee, different in every country (for example 3% + $0.25 in the US and 4% + £0.20 in the UK).
- **Regulatory operating fee:** 0% in the US and Australia, 0.48% in the UK, 0.50% in Canada, 0.05% in India, and it varies by country in the EU.
- **Offsite Ads fee:** 15% (or 12% for some sellers) on orders that come from an Etsy-promoted ad.
- **Currency conversion fee:** 2.5% when your listing currency differs from your payout currency.

There is no monthly fee, no setup fee, and no store subscription on standard Etsy seller accounts. Every cost comes from actual activity.

## The Listing Fee

The listing fee is $0.20 per item. It is flat, so it does not care whether you sell a $5 craft supply or a $500 furniture piece. You pay it when you publish, when a listing auto-renews every four months, and when a sold listing renews for the next buyer. A shop with 100 listings that renew twice a year pays 100 x 2 x $0.20 = $40 a year before any sales happen.

## The Transaction Fee

The transaction fee is 6.5% of the order total. Etsy computes it on the item price plus shipping charged to the buyer plus gift wrap, not just the item price. On a $50 item with $5 shipping the base is $55, so the fee is $55 x 6.5% = $3.58. This fee applies to every completed order, in every market.

## Payment Processing by Country

Payment processing is where countries differ most. In 2026 the rates are:

- **US:** 3% + $0.25
- **UK:** 4% + £0.20
- **EU:** 4% + €0.30
- **Canada:** 3% + C$0.25
- **Australia:** 3% + A$0.25 on domestic orders, 4% + A$0.25 on international orders
- **India:** 5% + ₹25

Processing is charged on the same base as the transaction fee. On a $55 US order it is 3% x $55 + $0.25 = $1.90. On a ₹1,000 Indian order it is 5% x ₹1,000 + ₹25 = ₹75, which shows how much the flat portion matters on smaller orders.

## The Regulatory Operating Fee

Some markets add a small regulatory operating fee on top. The UK charges 0.48%, Canada 0.50%, India 0.05%, and the US and Australia charge nothing. In the EU the rate varies by country, so it is marked unverified in our data — check your own country's rate before you rely on it. On a £55 UK order the 0.48% fee is about £0.26, small but never zero.

## Offsite Ads

If an order arrives through an Etsy Offsite Ad (Google, Instagram, Facebook, and similar placements), Etsy adds 15% of the order total, or 12% for sellers who qualify for the reduced rate. On a $55 order that is $8.25 on top of the normal fees, pushing total Etsy costs from about $5.68 to about $13.93. Offsite Ads fees apply for 30 days after the ad click, so track the window carefully during a sale period.

## Currency Conversion Fee

A 2.5% currency conversion fee applies when the currency you list in differs from the currency you get paid in. If you list in USD but receive payouts in another currency, every order pays this. Sellers who match their listing and payout currency avoid it entirely. Check the setting in your Etsy payment account rather than assuming the default fits you.

## Worked Example: A $55 US Sale

Take a $50 item with $5 shipping to a US buyer:

- Listing fee: $0.20
- Transaction fee: 6.5% x $55 = $3.58
- Payment processing: 3% x $55 + $0.25 = $1.90
- Regulatory fee: $0.00

Total Etsy fees: about **$5.68**, which is roughly 10.3% of the order. You keep $49.32 before your COGS and shipping costs. Offsite Ads on the same order would add another $8.25.

## Common Mistakes

- Forgetting the listing fee on every sale when you calculate margin.
- Using the item price alone instead of item price plus shipping as the fee base.
- Assuming the US processing rate applies in every country.
- Ignoring the regulatory fee in the UK, Canada, or India because it looks tiny.
- Not noticing the 2.5% currency conversion fee on cross-currency payouts.
- Treating an Offsite Ads order like a normal order — the fee stacks, it does not replace anything.

## Tips to Keep Fees Down

- Price with the full fee stack in mind, not just the 6.5% transaction fee.
- Consolidate variations into fewer listings to cut renewal costs.
- Deactivate listings that never sell so they stop renewing at $0.20 each.
- Match your listing currency to your payout currency to skip the 2.5% conversion fee.
- Check whether you qualify for the 12% Offsite Ads rate instead of 15%.
- Run each new price through the calculator before you publish it.
